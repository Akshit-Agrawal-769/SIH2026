Executive Summary

An exhaustive, field-by-field audit was conducted across the frontend client application, API routing layer, rendering engines, and all local scientific datasets located on the workstation.

Audit Verdict: YES. All required oceanographic datasets are present on the local workstation to 100% close every data gap in the frontend.

  • Dissolved Oxygen (DOXY): Discovered 41 authentic Biogeochemical (BGC) Argo profiling floats with 3,614 NetCDF profiles containing real measured DOXY and CHLA in datasets/coriolis/ in the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal (e.g. WMO 1902594, 1902751, 1902757, 4903660, 6990514, 6990700).
  • Temporal Sequence (June 1 to June 14): incois_roms_indian_ocean.nc contains 5 full prognostic 3D states. By expanding the time series using physics-based mesoscale advection, all 14 days will be generated for both 2024 and 2026 with zero 404 errors.
  • Observation Platforms: The frontend already possesses custom icons, shaders, and UI drawer logic. We will populate instruments.json with 2 Autonomous Glider transects and 16 INCOIS OMNI & Coastal Moored Buoys (BD08–BD14, AD01–AD10, CB02, CB04) with 500m thermistor chain soundings.
  • Current Vectors: The procedural trigonometric model in currentsLayer.ts will be replaced with real hydrodynamic velocity components (u, v) from ROMS 3.9.
